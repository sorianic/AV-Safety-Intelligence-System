AV Safety Intelligence System (SIR)

A proof-of-concept exploring how fragmented safety information could be connected into usable operational intelligence.

Concept / Proof of Concept

The Concept

Safety-critical organizations can generate enormous amounts of information across incidents, investigations, corrective actions, hazard analyses, regulations, research, and operational systems. SIR explores what could happen if those sources were connected rather than treated as isolated records.

INGEST → NORMALIZE → CONNECT → ANALYZE → ACT → LEARN
The objective is not to replace safety professionals or automate safety decisions. The objective is to help people find relationships, recognize patterns, investigate efficiently, and make better-informed decisions.

Role of AI

SIR treats AI as a supporting capability rather than a decision-maker. Potential applications include information normalization, relationship discovery, similarity identification, research retrieval, pattern recognition, and drafting assistance.

The concept is LLM-agnostic. A production implementation could use an appropriate model based on organizational security, privacy, performance, governance, cost, and deployment requirements.

AI can help organize, retrieve, compare, and surface information. Human professionals retain judgment and decision authority.
